Culture is more than just a list of the past, it is the music we hum, the meals we gather around, the stories we pass down by word and hand, and the unspoken bonds that tie us together. Here, we explore the vibrant traditions, age-old customs, and everyday artistry of philippines. Whether you are tracing your own roots or discovering a world outside your own for the first time, step inside, look around, and discover the heartbeat of our heritage.

BAYANIHAN

The spirit of helping neighbors, once shown by carrying a whole house together.

MANO PO

Taking an elder's hand and touching it to your forehead as a sign of respect

BARO'T SAYA

The traditional dress for women, with a blouse, skirt, and shawl.

HARANA

A man singing under a woman's window at night as a form of courtship.

PAMAMANHIKAN

The groom's family formally visits the bride's family to ask for her hand.

SIMBANG GABI

Nine early morning masses before Christmas, a beloved Filipino tradition

NOCHE BUEANA

The big family feast on Christmas Eve at midnight.

MINIATURE

Small hand-painted replicas of jeepneys, a popular souvenir craft.

PASIYAM

Nine days of prayer for someone who passed away.

BANIG WEAVING

Woven sleeping mats from pandan, buri, or tikog leaves from Basey, Samar and Sulu.

PAGTATAWAS

A traditional folk healing ritual to find out the cause of an illness.

TINIKLING

The national dance where dancers step in and out of clapping bamboo poles.

BAHAY KUBO

The traditional nipa hut made from bamboo and nipa palm.

BAYBAYIN

The ancient Filipino writing system, now popular in tattoos and art.

KULINTANG

A set of gongs played in Mindanao, especially by Maranao and Maguindanao people.

RONDALLA

An ensemble of string instruments like bandurria and guitar.

PAROL

The star-shaped Christmas lantern that lights up Filipino homes.

ANTING-ANTING

A traditional amulet believed to bring protection and luck.

KALESA

A horse-drawn carriage still seen in Intramuros and Vigan.

SALAKOT

Woven hats from buri palm leaves, used by farmers all over Luzon.

RATTAN FURNITURE

Chairs and tables made from rattan vines, famous in Cebu.

WOOD CARVING

Ifugao bulul statues, Paete carvings, and religious santos from Laguna and Bohol.

PUNI

Folded palm leaves made into flowers, birds, and decors for fiestas in Bulacan.

CALLADO

Embroidered lace and piña embroidery from Lumban, Laguna.

POTTERY

Unglazed clay jars and pots from Vigan, Ilocos Sur.

KIPING

Colorful rice wafer decors shaped like leaves, made in Lucban, Quezon for Pahiyas Festival.

TAKA

Handmade papier-mâché horses and animals from Paete, Laguna.

T'BOLI BRASS

Brass bells, bracelets, and beaded accessories from Mindanao tribes.

SANTO

Hand-carved wooden saints from Betis, Pampanga and Paete, Laguna.

SHELL CRAFT

Jewelry and accessories made from shells and pearls from Cebu and Palawan.
