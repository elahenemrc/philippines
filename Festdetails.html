Festivals are the heartbeat of our community a time when ancient traditions spill into the streets in a burst of music, dance, and shared gratitude. From grand religious processions and harvest celebrations to vibrant street parades, explore the calendar of events that bring us filipino's together year after year.

ATI-ATIHAN

Kalibo, Aklan: Known as the Mother of All Philippine Festivals. People paint faces, wear colorful costumes, and dance for Santo Niño.

SINULOG

Cebu City: Street dancing for Santo Niño with the famous two steps forward, one step back movement.

PANAGBENGA

Baguio City: The Flower Festival every February, with flower-covered floats and street dancing.

DINAGYANG

Iloilo City: A loud warrior dance competition and street party for Santo Niño.

MASSKARA

Bacolod City: The Festival of Smiles, with dancers wearing colorful smiling masks.

KADAYAWAN

Davao City: A harvest festival celebrating life, culture, and the bountiful harvest of Davao.

PAHIYAS

Lucban, Quezon: Houses decorated with colorful kiping, fruits, and vegetables for San Isidro Labrador.

MORIONES

Marinduque: Men wearing Roman soldier masks reenact the story of Longinus during Holy Week.

KAAMULAN

Bukidnon: A gathering of the seven tribes of Bukidnon to celebrate their culture and rituals.

HIGANTES

Angono, Rizal: Parade of giant papier-mache figures made to look like farmers and fishermen.

FLORES DE MAYO

A May procession of beautiful sagalas honoring the Virgin Mary.

GIANT LANTERNS

San Fernando, Pampanga: Christmas lanterns as big as houses light up the night.

PINTAFLORES

San Carlos City, Negros: Dancers with painted flowers on their bodies celebrate the city festival.

T'NALAK

South Cotabato: A celebration of the T'boli people's colorful T'nalak weaving.

TRASLACION

Quiapo, Manila: Massive procession of the Black Nazarene

SANDUGO

Tagbilaran, Bohol: Commemorates blood compact between Datu Sikatuna and Legazpi

PENAFRANCIA

Naga City, Camarines Sur: One of Asia's largest Marian celebrations, fluvial procession

HERMOSA

Zamboanga City: Vinta regatta, street dances for Our Lady of the Pillar
